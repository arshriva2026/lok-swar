SLIDE 1: PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING
================================================================================
HEADER:
PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING

SUBTITLE:
THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING

ON-SLIDE CONTENT (BULLET POINTS):

1. What is the Core Problem?
   - The "Voice-to-Action" Disconnect: Non-literate, vernacular rural citizens are completely excluded from digital governance because existing portals mandate typed formal English or standard Hindi.
   - Resource allocation by district administrations is slow, opaque, and disconnected from on-ground urgency.

2. Who is Affected?
   - Rural & Tribal Citizens: 68%+ rural population in constituencies like Sundargarh speaking regional dialects (Bhojpuri, Sambalpuri/Odia, Sadri, Bengali).
   - Vulnerable Populations: Illiterate farmers, women, daily wage workers, and remote panchayats cut off during monsoon floods.
   - District Planners & Engineers: Lack verified, real-time ground truth and make budget decisions blind to spatial severity.

3. Why is it Important?
   - Unresolved civic issues escalate into life-threatening emergencies (e.g., washed-out culverts blocking ambulances, poisoned drinking water wells).
   - Massive Misallocation of Public Funds: Millions in constituency budgets are spent on low-priority works while critical lifelines rot without tracking.

4. What is Wrong with the Current Approach?
   - High Digital Literacy Barrier: Existing portals (CPGRAMS, state apps) demand typed text, complex forms, and bureaucratic terminology.
   - "Ghost Infrastructure": Official registries claim assets are "completed/operational" while ground reality is defunct.
   - No Mathematical Prioritization: Grievances lie in a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) queue or depend on political influence rather than objective need.
   - Slow & Unverified Audits: Physical inspections take weeks and lack tamper-proof aerial evidence.

5. What Specific Part of the PS are We Addressing?
   - Solving "People's Priorities" end-to-end: Bridging the complete gap from hyper-local dialect voice intake to cross-registry evidence fusion, 12-factor MAUT priority ranking, and 0-1 Knapsack MILP budget allocation.

SLIDE 2: PROPOSED SOLUTION
================================================================================
HEADER:
PROPOSED SOLUTION

SUBTITLE:
OUR SOLUTION

ON-SLIDE CONTENT (BULLET POINTS):

1. What are You Building?
   - LOK SWAR (लोक स्वर): An AI-powered civic intelligence platform featuring multi-dialect voice intake, GIS spatial clustering, autonomous drone verification, and mathematical budget allocation.

2. How Does it Solve the Problem?
   - Zero-Typing Voice Intake: Citizens simply tap and speak in their local dialect. Audio is transcribed, translated to administrative English/Hindi, and mapped to statutory schemes (PMGSY, Jal Jeevan, SDRF).
   - Multi-Source Evidence Fusion: Corroborates citizen claims against official registries to detect discrepancies ("Ghost Infrastructure").
   - Autonomous Drone Telemetry & CV: Dispatches automated UAV survey flights with Computer Vision to detect road cracks, washed-out culverts, and generate SHA-256 tamper-proof evidence.
   - Mathematical Budget Optimizer: Employs an integrated 0-1 Knapsack MILP solver with a 12-factor MAUT formula to allocate constituency budgets (₹10 Cr pool) while guaranteeing rural equity.

3. What is Your Core Idea?
   - "From Dialect to Development": Demystifying governance by transforming raw, unstructured rural voice notes into auditable, prioritized civic infrastructure projects.

4. What Makes Your Approach Different?
   - Dialect Normalization Engine: Recognizes regional colloquialisms ('बा', 'नइखे', 'गइल', 'पुलवा', 'चापाकल') that crash standard translation tools.
   - Tri-Factor Ground Triangulation: Citizen Voice (Demand) + Official Registry (Data) + Autonomous Drone CV (Physical Proof).
   - Algorithmic Fairness: Replaces political bias with Multi-Attribute Utility Theory (MAUT) and guaranteed rural quotas.
   - Zero-Failure High Availability: Seamless MongoDB with local JSON fallback, live Fast2SMS OTP, and DigiLocker e-KYC.

Citizen Input -> Multilingual Processing -> AI Scheme Mapping -> Registry & Drone Analysis -> 0-1 Knapsack Budget Output

   - Tri-Factor Evidence Fusion: Citizen Claim vs. Official Registry Discrepancy

   - 0-1 Knapsack MILP Budget Optimization (₹10 Cr pool with rural equity quotas)
